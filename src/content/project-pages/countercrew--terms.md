The iPhone and iPad app uses [Apple's Standard End User License Agreement](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/) for its app license. This page explains how the restaurant workspace is intended to be used; it does not replace that agreement or your employer's procedures.

## Authorized business workspaces

Create a business workspace only when you are authorized to manage it. Add staff accounts only for people authorized to access that business. Keep credentials and owner recovery keys private. Use only content and photographs you are authorized to provide; do not upload unlawful, abusive or unrelated personal content.

## Restaurant responsibility

Starter checklists are editable examples. Operators must adapt them to their approved recipes, equipment, food safety procedures and staff training. A completed checkbox, photo or timer does not certify safety, cleanliness or regulatory compliance. Follow the restaurant's approved process and escalate unsafe conditions.

Work timing is declared operational information. It is separate from task completion and is not a payroll time clock or an automatic employment decision. The app does not place supplier orders or send payments.

## Your records and choices

The [privacy policy](/countercrew/privacy/) explains access and retention. You can [delete your account in the app](/countercrew/delete-account/). Owners can transfer control to an eligible GM before leaving a team. Independent businesses cannot access each other's workspace through the app.
