**The short version:** FHS: To do list is an internal checklist for authorized PSM LLC store teams. It has no advertising or tracking. Identifying task-completion details and related task photos are automatically deleted after seven days.

## Who operates the app

FHS: To do list is offered by Bhavya Patel for PSM LLC store operations. Questions and privacy requests may be sent to [patelbhavya216@gmail.com](mailto:patelbhavya216@gmail.com).

## Information we collect

- **Account information:** name, username, assigned store locations, role, and authentication identifiers.
- **Checklist activity:** tasks selected or completed, completion status, completion time, and the completing person's name.
- **Task evidence:** a new camera photo only for applicable team-facing Closing cleanup tasks, such as sweeping, mopping, taking out trash, washing dishes, and similar cleaning work. Prep, slicing, Opening, Day Shift, and GM tasks do not require photos. The app does not offer photo-library or file uploads.
- **Device and security information:** push-notification token, session identifiers, request identifiers, and limited security or audit records needed to operate and protect the service.
- **Content entered while using the app:** task comments or review results when those fields apply.

## How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate authorized users, show the correct store checklists, coordinate task completion, provide proof of completion, deliver checklist notifications and progress-only Live Activities, manage authorized accounts, prevent misuse, and maintain the service.

## Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking. Data is processed by Supabase as the hosted authentication, database, and private file-storage provider. Apple processes notification delivery through Apple Push Notification service and processes App Store distribution under Apple's own terms.

## Retention and deletion

The person's name attached to a completed task, the exact task-completion record and time, and related task photos are automatically deleted after seven days. Anonymous store-and-day totals may remain because they do not identify a person or retain a photo or exact task record. Active account information is kept while access is needed. A deactivated account is anonymized after the configured seven-day cleanup window, subject to reliable service operation and backup lifecycle.

## Who can see information

Each person sees only their own account. Authorized team users see the shared checklist for stores assigned to them. GMs may see task progress, task-specific completion stamps and photos, and the minimum account roster necessary for stores they manage. Owners may see the same information across PSM LLC locations and may manage GMs and team accounts. Access is enforced on the server.

## Notifications and camera

Notification permission is optional and can be changed in iPhone Settings. Notifications are intended only for team users, with delivery paused from 12:00–2:00 PM and 6:00–7:30 PM Eastern. Camera access is requested only when current task evidence is required. Lock Screen Live Activities show checklist progress counts, not employee names or photos.

## Security

We use encrypted network connections, private file storage, role- and store-based access controls, protected iOS credential storage, and server-side authorization. No system can be guaranteed perfectly secure, but access and retention controls are designed to limit exposure.

## Your choices

You may decline notifications or stop a Live Activity without losing checklist access. For account correction, access removal, or a privacy request, contact your GM or owner, or email [patelbhavya216@gmail.com](mailto:patelbhavya216@gmail.com). We may need to verify that the requester controls the relevant account.

## Children

The app is an authorized workplace tool and is not directed to children.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially, the effective date above will be updated and an appropriate notice will be provided.
