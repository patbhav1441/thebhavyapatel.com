CounterCrew is preparing for public App Store release. For technical help or a privacy request, contact [patelbhavya216@gmail.com](mailto:patelbhavya216@gmail.com).

## Set up a restaurant

Choose **Start your restaurant** at Sign In, enter your business and first location, choose an owner username/password, and save your recovery key privately. Review the twelve starter tasks and their times, then use **Overview → Add or remove accounts** to add your GM and staff. GMs and owners can use **Edit restaurant checklists** or **Store tools → Checklist templates** where authorized.

Managers share new staff sign-in details privately. A staff member should change the temporary password from the account menu. Employees begin their shift at the assigned restaurant, complete the required task confirmation/photo, and use **End Shift** when finished. Finishing a task's work timer does not submit its completion.

## Sign-in or device trouble

Owners can use **Forgot password?** with the recovery key saved at signup. Staff should contact their managing GM or owner. Keep passwords and recovery keys private; support will not ask you to email either. A designated-device error requires an authorized owner to review/reset work-device access. Do not create a second staff identity to bypass it.

## Report a problem or inappropriate content

Use a task's **Needs help** control for an operational problem. Contact the GM or owner for incorrect assignments or team content. If the concern involves your manager or a privacy issue, contact the developer directly at the email above. Include the app version/build, a description and approximate time; do not send passwords, recovery keys or unnecessary employee/customer information. Restaurant managers can remove staff access within their authority.

## Account deletion

Use [the in-app deletion steps](/countercrew/delete-account/). Daily evidence expires after seven days; private employment records follow the [privacy policy](/countercrew/privacy/).
