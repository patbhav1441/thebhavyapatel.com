## Play imported videos

1. Open **Library** and choose **Add reels**.
2. Select videos from **Photos** or **Files**. Reelo copies the selected files into its local library with their original audio.
3. Tap **Play your loop** for continuous playback. Use repeat-all, repeat-one, shuffle, and skip controls, or create a playlist in the **Playlists** tab.

Local video playback works without an Instagram account. A saved link by itself is a bookmark; attach a matching video file to play it in the local player.

## Scan Instagram Saved links

1. Open Reelo’s **Instagram** tab and sign in directly on Instagram’s website. Enter passwords and verification codes only on that page.
2. Open your Instagram profile’s **Saved** page. Some website layouts also show **All posts**. Reelo remembers the Saved page on this device.
3. Tap **Scan Saved** and keep the tab open while it scrolls. Collected links are saved in your library, including partial results if the scan stops.
4. Tap **Play saved Reel links** to stream the explicit Reel links through Instagram’s web player. Use the queue’s repeat, sound, shuffle, and skip controls.

You do not have to download and upload each video for this web-playback mode. It needs an internet connection and an active Instagram web session. It does not create offline copies or promise background playback. Older post links may contain photos or videos and are excluded from the explicit Reel queue.

## If a scan or Reel stops

Instagram controls login, verification, availability, and the content its website loads. Complete any verification on Instagram, reload the page if necessary, open Saved, and scan again. Duplicate links are merged. Instagram can stop loading before the full collection is reached, so the count is the number of links found rather than a guarantee that every Saved item was imported.

If a particular Reel is unavailable or its player does not start, use **Next**. Changes to Instagram’s website can affect this experimental feature. Reelo does not use an official Saved-collection API.

## Sound and storage

Check the player’s sound control, your iPhone volume, and the active Bluetooth or audio output. Imported videos retain the audio present in the selected file. Instagram playback uses the audio Instagram makes available for that post and account.

Imported files use device storage. Removing a video removes its local imported file and playlist references. Removing a playlist leaves the videos in your library. The Instagram tab’s menu provides sign-out for Reelo’s website session.
