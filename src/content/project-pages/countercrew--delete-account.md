## Delete from the app

1. Open the **Account and store menu** and choose **Delete account**. Staff who have not begun their shift can use **Delete account** on the shift screen.
2. Enter your current password.
3. Choose **Delete my account**, then **Permanently delete account**.

The app ends account access and anonymizes the profile. Authentication credentials and sign-in identities are removed, with a service retry if necessary. You are returned to Sign In. Deletion also revokes the saved owner recovery key; it cannot restore a deleted account.

## If you are the only owner

A business with active staff needs an owner to keep operating. In the deletion screen, choose an active GM from your business and confirm **Transfer ownership**. The GM must first end their active shift. They receive full owner access; your own account remains active until you delete it. If no eligible GM is available, contact support to resolve ownership before deletion. An empty public workspace closes when its owner deletes their account.

## What happens to records

Self-deletion immediately ends memberships, anonymizes the profile, and removes employment work metrics and warnings. Shared daily task records/photos remain only within their existing seven-day retention window. Business configuration may remain for the team, and non-personal audit identifiers can remain to preserve record integrity. See the [privacy policy](/countercrew/privacy/) for details.
