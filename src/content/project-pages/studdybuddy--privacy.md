## Purpose of this draft

This record is an editing framework, not a published privacy policy. Before publication, the product owner must verify every category of account, profile, message, media, device, analytics, notification, permission, location, AI-provider, and backend data used by the actual application.

## Review gates

The final document must identify processing purposes, service providers, retention periods, deletion behavior, user choices, age restrictions, security practices, policy-change handling, and a working contact method. It must not make absolute claims that the implementation cannot prove.
