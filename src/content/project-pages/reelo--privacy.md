This policy describes Reelo for iPhone and its support pages, operated by Bhavya Pragneshbhai Patel. It is effective November 5, 2026.

## Local videos and saved links

Reelo copies videos you select from Photos or Files into its local app storage. It stores video names, Instagram post links, playlist names, playlist order, and the location of imported files on your device. Reelo does not upload this library to an app-operated server.

Local files may be included in device backups according to your Apple and device settings. Reelo does not operate an account system or a separate cloud-sync service.

## Instagram website sessions

The optional Instagram tab loads Instagram’s website inside the app. When you use it, your device communicates directly with Instagram and Meta. Login information you enter is sent to Instagram’s website, not to a Reelo-operated server. Instagram may collect information and use cookies or other website technologies under [Meta’s privacy policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/).

The app uses persistent WebKit website storage so that Instagram can maintain its login session on this device. Reelo’s automation does not read password-field values, cookies, authentication tokens, or private API responses. It reads rendered saved-post links and associated displayed text, stores those bookmarks locally, and controls rendered HTML video playback.

Web mode streams Instagram content; it does not copy the streamed video or soundtrack into Reelo’s local video library. Instagram’s own login, verification, content restrictions, and service behavior still apply.

## Analytics and advertising

Reelo does not include its own analytics, advertising SDK, or tracking server. Embedded Instagram pages are provided by Meta and may include Meta’s own analytics or tracking technologies. The support website’s hosting provider, Cloudflare, may process basic network-request information to deliver and secure the website.

## Photos and Files access

Reelo uses the system Photos and Files pickers. It receives the items you select for import. It does not scan your entire photo library in the background. Instagram Saved scanning runs in the open Instagram tab when you start it.

## Your choices and deletion

You can remove reels, imported videos, and playlists inside the app. Removing a playlist leaves its videos in the library. Signing out from the Instagram tab clears the relevant Instagram/Meta website session data within Reelo; it does not sign you out of a separate browser or the Instagram app.

Removing Reelo from your device removes its local app storage. You manage existing device backups through Apple’s backup settings. Content and account information held by Instagram must be managed through Instagram and Meta.

## Support communications

If you email support, the email address, message, and attachments you choose to send are used to respond to your request. Avoid sending passwords, verification codes, authentication tokens, or private media. You can ask us to delete a support conversation, subject to any retention obligations.

Reelo is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Instagram or Meta.
