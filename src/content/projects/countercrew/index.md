## Restaurant work, clearly organized

CounterCrew is an iPhone and iPad app preparing for public App Store release. Independent and franchise restaurant operators can create their own private business workspace without an invitation from us, then add GMs, AGMs, Shift Leaders and Team Members.

The app supports sandwich and sub shops, coffee and breakfast shops, burger and chicken restaurants, bowl and burrito kitchens, and dessert and ice-cream shops. It is an independent product and is not sponsored by or affiliated with a restaurant chain.

## From opening to closing

Each new restaurant receives twelve editable example tasks across Opening, Prep, Day Shift and Closing. Managers should review the instructions, times and evidence requirements against their restaurant's approved procedures before real use. Owners monitor progress; staff begin a shift to perform work.

Task completion records the employee and server-confirmed time. Applicable photo tasks use a new in-app camera photo. Begin Task and Finish Work record declared work time separately from task completion. Help requests and team updates keep unfinished work visible.

Daily checklists reset at midnight New York time. Detailed daily records and photos last seven days. A separate minimal work-metrics projection lasts up to ninety days or until employment ends; private warnings remain for active employment. These are operational records, not payroll or automatic employment decisions.

## Private to your business

Businesses have separate server-enforced access. Owners can manage their team; GMs can manage the positions within their authority. No advertising, cross-app tracking or AI training on your business records is included.
