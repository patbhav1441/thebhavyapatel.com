StuddyBuddy explores a focused question: how might students find study partners who fit the way they actually work?

The current product direction combines course context, shared interests, study preferences, and communication or meeting choices. Social and messaging features are part of the concept, as is the possibility of carefully bounded AI study support.

Because this product may involve accounts, user-generated content, and in-person meetings, the safety, moderation, privacy, deletion, and support model must be confirmed before any legal documents are published or the product is described as App Store ready.
