## Your favorites, on repeat

Reelo plays your personal video library as a continuous playlist. Choose videos through the iPhone Photos or Files picker, then use repeat-all, repeat-one, shuffle, and playback controls. Imported files retain their original video and audio without transcoding.

An optional, experimental Instagram tab opens Instagram’s website. Sign in there, open your profile’s Saved page, and tap **Scan Saved**. Reelo scrolls the rendered page and stores the post links Instagram loads. **Play saved Reel links** streams explicit Reel links in Instagram’s own web player and advances through the queue.

This website mode requires an internet connection and the Instagram tab to remain open. Instagram may change its website, require verification, limit loading, or make a post unavailable. A scan can preserve a partial collection; it does not guarantee every Saved item is found. It does not download Instagram video files or soundtracks into the local library.

Reelo is in private testing. An App Store release is being prepared; public availability has not been announced.

## Help and privacy
