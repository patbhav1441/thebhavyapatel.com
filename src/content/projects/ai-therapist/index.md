Despite its working project name, this is a mental-wellness companion concept—not medical treatment, a licensed therapist, an emergency service, or a replacement for professional care.

Before this concept advances, its crisis handling, age policy, AI-provider disclosure, data sensitivity, and user-safety model need dedicated review. No clinical outcomes are claimed.
