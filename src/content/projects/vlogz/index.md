Vlogz explores a more intimate alternative to broad public social feeds: short daily video updates shared within friend groups, with conversation and lightweight reactions close at hand.

The product is still a concept. Its actual storage, moderation, reporting, blocking, retention, account deletion, age policy, and third-party processing behavior must be defined before legal documents or availability claims are published.
