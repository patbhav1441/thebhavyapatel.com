This project explores how different market signals can be organized into a consistent experimental workflow. The legacy portfolio describes fundamental, technical, and sentiment analysis alongside classical and quantum-oriented modeling experiments; the precise architecture and results still require artifact-level review.

Project outputs are informational and experimental. They are not financial advice, a guarantee of performance, or a recommendation to buy or sell securities.
